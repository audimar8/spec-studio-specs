# Perf fixture

A large spec generated to exercise the parser, applyOps, the spec graph
and the renderer. ~3,000 lines by design. Content is intentionally
repetitive; the shape is the test, not the words.

## 1. Problem Statement

The app must stay responsive on a 3,000-line spec. Parsing, applying an
edit op and sending the graph's related blocks to Claude must all stay
well under a second.
